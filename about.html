Introduction

KIIT (Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology) is a premier university located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. It is renowned for its high-quality education across Engineering, Management, Law, Biotechnology, Medical Sciences, Media & Design and many more disciplines.
